# The unit dialect (#1076) — plan

**Anchor:** hale-lang/hale#1076 (the thread and its sketch); the comment on #1212 of 2026-09-28, "The unit dialect, concretely", which is the committed form (where it and the #1076 sketch differ, it wins); #1212's Final direction, which makes the dialect an extension with a review of its own; the exact catalogue already on `main` (`spec/units.md`, `crates/hale-types/src/unit_graph.rs`, #1345); and the F.40 phase-4 plan (`f40/phase4-plan`), whose line S this work leans on.

**What this document is.** The plan for delivering the dialect, written for review before any pane starts. It is the first piece of work after F.40 and is ordered to land directly behind phase 4's close. Every size was measured on the tree at 89147e00 by two read-only surveys (the syntax and its tooling; typing, representation and lowering). Revision 2 folds the outside review's finding (PR #1367): a ratio product has a denomination of its own, stated in §3 and decision 2, so the money example's separate bindings are exact and its rounding happens where the policy is.

## 1. Where the tree is

- **The arithmetic core exists and has no consumer.** `unit_graph.rs` closes a catalogue of unit identities and exact rational equations, proves every cycle multiplies to one (or returns the witnessed cycle), answers a conversion as one reduced `p/q` with its path, and computes the gcd denomination with its witnesses. It is registered (`unit_catalogue`, Canonical). What a consumer needs and it lacks: a denomination that can be stored and compared (today's borrows the graph and hides its scale), a conversion to machine integers, points and origins, and a type for a loss policy.
- **Duration literals exist, fixed in the lexer.** `500ms` lexes to one token already scaled to nanoseconds, so the spelled unit is gone before the parser runs. The suffix set is closed (`ns us ms s m h d`), `5m` is minutes whatever is in scope, `1d` is unreachable (it lexes as a decimal), and the compound form the spec documents (`1h30m`) fails by reading. The tree holds 571 such literals, 514 of them arguments of `sleep`; by suffix `ms` 408, `s` 35, `h` 5, `ns` 3, and none of `us`, `m` or `d`.
- **`Time` and `Duration` are primitives from the parser on.** They are in the parser's primitive-name table, so a type position never reaches a declaration. Their algebra is one function in the checker (`binop_ty`: `Duration ± Duration`, `Int * Duration`, `Duration / Int`, `Time ± Duration`, `Time - Time`; everything else refused) and a second copy in codegen's operator routing. 73 sites match the two primitives by name of the primitive and 108 more by codegen's representation type; about 25 predicates enumerate "the scalar types" and disagree on whether `Time` is one. There is no conversion from a `Duration` to an `Int` except a stdlib call, and 13 stdlib signatures take or return the two types, all nanoseconds at the C boundary.
- **A type declaration is an alias, a struct or an enum.** An alias is transparent by decision (GH #759): its name does not survive resolution. There is no nominal scalar, no range on a type, no literal polymorphism (an integer literal is an `Int`), no constant folding, and no integer width other than `Int`.
- **Fallibility has the shape the dialect needs.** `expr or <disposition>` exists with substitute, handler, raise and discard forms; the checker records a row per fallible call and lowering reads it; the bounded containers' `push`, `set` and `at` are the precedent for a fallible operation that is not a stdlib call.
- **Lowering does not read expression types.** The typed-body table carries rows for specific decisions (generic calls, monomorphs, fallible calls, accumulators, conformance), and codegen derives a representation type for itself. A conversion the checker decides has to reach lowering as a row.
- **What is frozen.** `shape_hash` renders no type or primitive name, so it does not move when `Time` and `Duration` become declarations or when a program declares a quantity. What does depend on them: a topic's shape string, where a `Duration` field is the tag `u`, a `Time` field `t`, and any field that is not a primitive `struct` (the string is compiled into the binary and hashed for the observer protocol); the bus wire's eight-byte copy; routing keys in nanoseconds; the FFI classes.
- **The syntax is mostly free.** `unit NAME = …;` at top level, `quantity`, `point` and `distinct` after `=` (the way `enum` is recognized there today), and a `{ range: …; }` block after a type are all parse errors today. Two forms collide: `d.in(s)` is refused because `in` is a hard keyword, and `or floor` parses as "substitute the value of `floor`". Unit names in expression position share a namespace with locals today, and `s`, `m` and `h` are among the commonest local names in the tree.

## 2. What the dialect is, and is not

The committed form, in its own words: rows of five kinds (unit edges with a rational label, quantities pinned to a node, points over a quantity with an optional origin, identities, ranges); a rational graph per quantity, closed at the declaration set; every conversion the program uses reduced to one `p/q`; **widening is free and narrowing is named**: a conversion with `q = 1` is exact and implicit, and one with `q > 1`, or into a narrower range, is discharged by `or` at the site or by a policy on the target type, never by a default; `Time` and `Duration` are the first two declarations; every narrowing has a witness.

Not in this plan, as the committed form defers them: derived dimensions (`Bytes / Duration` as a rate), `Float` quantities and a physics catalogue, calendar projections. Not in this plan by its own decision: packing by width (the report says what a range fits in; no layout reads it yet), rounding functions declared by a program (a handler after `or` is already a program's own function), and a JSON form for a quantity (`Duration` has none today).

## 3. The design, as the compiler will hold it

**Rows (layer 2).** One family beside the catalogue, demanded from the snapshot like the target row: unit declarations and their equations (the catalogue's input, closed once); quantity rows (the component, the denomination as a unit and an integer multiple, the policy, the range); point rows (the quantity and the origin); identity rows (the base and the range); range rows (the parent and the bounds). A row's identity is its declaration's site, for the stdlib's declarations as for a program's. The laws are structural and each has a witness: a cycle that does not multiply to one names its two paths (the catalogue already produces this); two quantities never share a component, so each component has one quantity and its other types are denominations of it (decision 1); a range sits inside its parent's; an origin is denominated in its own quantity; a policy belongs to the family its narrowing admits; a literal suffix names one unit in scope.

**Types.** A quantity, a point, an identity or a range is a named type with a new kind, not a new variant of the checker's type: the 54 sites that read a type's kind and the six exhaustive matches are forced to answer for the new kinds, where a new variant would silently miss the 171 sites that match a primitive. Each is represented as the integer it counts, so the 345 codegen sites that handle an `Int` handle it unchanged. `Time` and `Duration` keep their representation class (their shape tags, their printing, their FFI classes), and the stdlib's two declarations are what gives them their algebra.

**A denomination no declaration names.** An expression's type is its quantity and a denomination, and the denomination need not be one a declaration pinned: `d.in(s)` is the quantity in seconds, a sum of two denominations is at their meet (the catalogue's gcd, which it already computes with its witnesses), and a ratio product is at the product of its operands' denominations. Such a type is synthesized, as a monomorph is: the same kind, keyed by its quantity and its denomination (a unit and an exact rational multiple), displayed as `Money in 1/10000 cent`, represented as the integer it counts, and carrying no policy and no range. It is an ordinary type for a `let` binding, an argument or a return, and it converts like any other: freely into a finer denomination, and into a coarser one only under a policy.

**The algebra**, stated once and read by the checker and by lowering:

| operands | result |
|---|---|
| quantity ± quantity (one component) | the quantity, at the finer denomination, exactly |
| quantity × `Int`, `Int` × quantity | the quantity |
| quantity × dimensionless quantity, either order | the quantity, at the product of the two denominations, exactly: nothing is rounded at the product |
| quantity ÷ `Int` | the quantity; a literal divisor other than 1 is a narrowing the checker can see (`spread / 2 or floor`), a runtime divisor is integer division |
| quantity ÷ quantity (one component) | `Int` |
| point − point | the quantity |
| point ± quantity, quantity + point | the point |
| point + point; any other product of two quantities; a quantity ÷ a dimensionless quantity; any arithmetic on an identity | refused |
| comparison, within one component or one identity | exact, at the finer denomination |
| anything across components or identities | refused, naming both declarations |

**Literals.** A unit literal keeps its magnitude and its unit's name to the checker. Unit names are a namespace of their own, as type names are, so a local named `s` neither shadows nor is shadowed by the unit `s`. A literal types at the quantity of its unit's component and is converted to that quantity's denomination at compile time; a literal that is not a whole number of the denomination it flows into is a narrowing like any other. Magnitudes are integers, as today. Literal arithmetic is not folded by the checker: `3s + 500ms` is two converted constants and an addition.

**Conversions are rows.** Wherever a value of one denomination or range meets another (an assignment, an argument, a return, an operand, `.in(u)`, `.split(u)`, a cast `T(x)`), the checker classifies the site and records it in the typed bodies: the factor, widening or narrowing, and the policy that discharges it. Lowering emits one multiplication or one division with the policy's rounding from the row, and decides nothing. The forms after `or` on a narrowing: a substitute value (the conversion is fallible: exact, or the substitute), a policy word (total: `floor`, `ceil`, `trunc`, `half_even`, `half_up` for a ratio; `clamp`, `wrap` for a range), or a handler or `raise`, which are today's dispositions. A type that carries `{ round: … }` supplies the policy for narrowings into it.

**The witness report.** `hale check --units` lists, per declaration of a quantity type, its denomination and the row that fixed it, every narrowing with the policy that discharged it and where the policy came from, the headroom of the representation at that denomination, and what its range fits in. It is a development report, outside the hashed model half, and stable enough to record and diff.

## 4. Steps

Each step is one PR that leaves `main` shippable and carries its spec text, its book text and its fragment, since the spec describes shipped behaviour. Shapes are F.40's (S a shadowed move, D a design the driver fixes in the brief, J a classified correction with its decision written first).

| step | work | shape · model | oracle |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | **The declaration layer: syntax, rows and laws.** A `unit` declaration (an edge, or a node alone); one new body for a `type` declaration covering `quantity T in D`, `point Q`, `distinct T`, and a refinement of a named type (`T in D { round: …; }`, `T { range: a..b; }`), with its clause block (`range`, `round`, `origin`); a unit literal for a suffix outside today's duration set (those stay as they are until U4); `expr.in(D)` as its own form, since `in` is a keyword. ## 5. Order, and living beside phase 4

The dialect takes one of the three pane slots when phase 4 frees one, and never two. U1 has almost no shared surface with phase 4: it is `hale-syntax`, a new module in `hale-types`, one cell in `snapshot.rs` (lane F's file: one commit, rebased), and one arm in each exhaustive match (`check.rs` and `codegen.rs` among them: one line each, landed between the owning lane's steps). U2 and U3 put their logic in a new module (`crates/hale-types/src/units.rs`) that `check.rs` calls at a handful of points (the operator table, the call arm, assignability, literals), so lane W's ownership of `check.rs` is respected by landing those call-ins as single commits after W4 has merged; their lowering arms go through lane S the same way. U4 waits for S8. The builtin error type a failed narrowing raises is injected the way the containers' are today and joins S8's table when that lands.

## 7. Decisions for Riley before the steps start

Each states a recommendation, and the steps proceed on them once this plan's review is in.

1. **One quantity per component.** The committed form's law is that quantities are disjoint components, and its example also declares `Bucket = quantity Int in 100ms { round: floor; }` beside `Duration`. Recommended reading: a component has one quantity, declared without a policy; a declaration over the same component with its own denomination is a boundary denomination of that quantity (`Bucket` is `Duration in 100ms { round: floor; }` spelled either way); a literal with no expected type is that quantity. A second policy-free quantity over one component is an error naming both.
2. **Scaling by a ratio** (`1_250_000 USD * 3bp`). Two things are needed, and the committed example states neither. The first is a unit's relation to the pure number (`unit pct = 100 bp;` fixes `bp` against `pct` only). Recommended: a unit may be defined against a number (`unit pct = 1/100;`), and the component that contains it is dimensionless. The second, which the review of this plan found, is what the product is. `1_234_567 USD * 3bp` is 37,037.01 cents, which no integer count of cents holds; rounding it at the product would be a default policy, and refusing it would refuse the example. Recommended: **the product is the quantity at the product of the two denominations** (here `Money in 1/10000 cent`, the value 370,370,100), exact by construction for literals and for runtime operands alike. The example's three lines then mean what their comments say: `fee` and `odd` are both exact bindings of that synthesized type, and the narrowing is at `let paid: Ledger = odd;`, with `q = 10000` and `half_even` from `Ledger`. A destination with no policy refuses it (`let bad: Money = odd;` is an error naming the factor and the missing policy; `odd.in(cent) or half_even` and `odd.in(cent) or 0` are the two ways to say what is meant). Each further ratio factor makes the denomination finer and the headroom smaller, which the report shows (decision 7). U3's acceptance pins the separated bindings with literal operands, the same lines with runtime operands (an amount and a rate read at run time), and the refusal. The alternative defers the three lines and the dimensionless component with them.
3. **A literal's spelling.** Recommended: the magnitude and the unit are adjacent (`3bp`, `1_250_000USD`), as duration literals are today. The committed example writes `1_250_000 USD` with a space; allowing the space makes two adjacent tokens an expression, which nothing else in the grammar is.
4. **The time catalogue's names.** Recommended, as the committed form writes them: `ns`, `us`, `ms`, `s`, `min`, `h`, `day`. Today's `m` for minutes (no use in the tree) and the unreachable `d` are removed in U4, which frees `m`, and the documented compound literal (`1h30m`: unused, and broken by reading) leaves the spec.
5. **A literal divisor is a narrowing for every quantity, `Duration` included** (`timeout / 2 or floor`). Recommended: uniform; the tree has about two such sites. The alternative exempts the two built-in types.
6. **Policy words after `or`.** `or floor` parses today as substituting the value of `floor`. Recommended: after a narrowing, the policy words are read as policies, and a value of that name is written in parentheses.
7. **Overflow.** A widening multiplies. Recommended for this plan: it follows `Int`'s arithmetic; a type with a range is checked against it, and the report shows the headroom at each denomination. A checked widening is a later decision with a measured cost.
8. **Printing.** Recommended: a quantity prints as its count and its denomination's unit, as a `Duration` prints nanoseconds today; printing in another unit is formatting, as the committed form says.
9. **On the wire.** A quantity field of a topic is its integer. Recommended: its shape tag names its denomination, a new tag beside the existing ones, so two processes that disagree by a factor disagree in the shape hash the observer protocol already compares; existing topics and tags are untouched. Identities and ranges are tagged as the `Int` they are.
10. **The stdlib's catalogues.** Time, and byte counts. `Bytes` is taken by the buffer type, so the committed example's `type Bytes = quantity Int in B` needs another name; recommended `ByteCount`.
11. **The thread.** The three changes relative to the #1076 sketch are stated on #1212 and not on #1076. Recommended: a comment there pointing at the committed form and at this plan, posted on Riley's word, since it is a conversation with an outside contributor.

## 8. Risks

- **A conversion the checker forgets** would be a wrong number, the bug this feature exists to remove. The control is structural: a mixed-denomination value is legal only at a position the checker has classified and recorded, and everything else is a located error or a missing required row.
- **`Time` and `Duration` reach 181 sites.** U4 changes who decides their algebra and their literals, and nothing about how they are represented; IR identity over the corpus and DNA is the gate.
- **Literal suffixes are short names.** Units get a namespace of their own, and a suffix two catalogues define is an error naming both, never a silent choice.
- **Shared files with phase 4.** One module of its own, call-ins as single commits through the owning lane, and U4 after S8.
- **The words.** Contextual by position; the tree's uses of `unit`, `point`, `range`, `round`, `origin` and `split` as identifiers keep parsing, and a test holds that.
