### Fixed
- A child that fails from a bus handler is recovered as one failing from `run()` is. After the supervisor's `restart(c)` its `birth()` and `run()` run again and its subscriptions are put back; after an absorb it stops and drops its subscriptions. Before, a restart re-ran nothing and an absorbed child kept handling messages. When the handler ran inside the child's own `run()`, the decision is carried out once that `run()` returns (#2028).

### Breaking
- A parent's publish to its one field child that declares a `closure` or a `birth_check` is no longer rewritten to a direct method call. It goes through the bus, so the child's handler runs at the parent's next drain or `yield`, not inside the publish statement. The spec always called the rewrite an optimization, observably the same up to timing. Code that read such a child's state right after publishing to it sees the update after a `yield`. Migration: `yield;` (or end the body) before reading the child's state, or have the child publish what the parent needs (#2028).
