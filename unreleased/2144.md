### Added

- `@vectorize` and `@vectorize(min_width: N)` on a fn: every innermost loop written in its body (an element chain's loop included) must be vectorized by the build's optimizer on the build's target, at least `N` lanes wide when given, or `hale build` fails at the loop with the target and the reason, in the program's terms beside LLVM's own words (GH #2134, F.62). `hale check` refuses first what no target vectorizes, at the place that stops the loop: a `break` or `return` beside the loop's own exit, a call to a fn with effects or with a loop of its own, an allocation, a locus built, a publish or send, and a `@vectorize` fn with no loop. The rest is the target's answer (a `Float` sum vectorizes on aarch64, not x86-64). It asks the optimizer for nothing and changes nothing the program computes. It is not checked at O1 (`--dev`, `HALE_DEV`, `hale test`), under AddressSanitizer or on wasm, and `hale build` says when it skipped. spec/semantics.md § Vectorized loops.
