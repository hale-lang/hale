### Added

- The face's next shape, at `/next` behind the shell's gate: three basic screens over the head's surface. **Organization** is the org chart as the graph states it (every position under what it sits under, its holders, whether its mandate is ratified, the positions the record proposes and the Board has not decided with their Review, a grafted organism as a child organization), with fill and open as the record's commands. **Board** is the handed Tasks and the Reviews as columns, with a verdict, taking a Task and reporting it done. **Knowledge** is every node the record holds by kind, with its bindings and edges, and propose, revise and retire. The shell at `/` is unchanged.
- Two reads of `HeadCommands` behind them, relayed by the head like the index: `Commands::org` (the org chart from the record's graph rows, so it answers wherever the record does) and `Commands::tasks` (the handed Tasks from the record and its Ledger as one, as the signed-in person, wherever the head reaches the Ledger; the HTTP tasks read answers only a trusted-local session).

### Fixed

- A verdict through the head's `Commands::verdict` on a purpose, a mandate, a hole, a hold, a batch or a design Review answered `stale_subject`: the pre-check admitted only an idea, concept, practice, task_concept or retirement proposal, so a face could ratify a practice and nothing else. Every proposal a Review decides is admitted now; a graph proposal, a family or a proposal whose document is not a knowledge document is judged on its Review row, and the organization's Review decides it as it decides the CLI's verdict.
