### The face: tasks for chart positions

- The board opens a Task for a position the chart states (`TaskOpen`), and whoever holds the position, a person or an agent, takes it, posts updates (`TaskNote`, a `task.noted` row), hands it to another position (`TaskHand`) and marks it done, with no organization or model involved. `TaskTake` and `TaskDone` accept a holder of the Task's position. Task rows carry `position`, `opened_by` and `result`, and history entries an update's `text`. The board shows the signed-in person's Tasks by default.
- Every org chart change in the face is one batch and one Board Review, including adding a position with its holder. It shows in the chart and under In review at once, and the face follows it until it settles.
- A small batch's Review question names its steps, and the reviews read no longer hides a batch's question.

Breaking: none
