### Changed

- **A checked sum of a fixed-width integer vectorizes** (GH #2162, F.65). A sum of `I32` … `U8` over a `bounded` or a fixed array, either `.map(it * 3).sum(U32(0))` or `for x in xs { s = s + x; }`, whose loop only computes, is now added up in 64 bits and checked once when the loop ends. Before, every add branched to the violation. An overflow is the same violation, now reported after the loop instead of at the element that crossed. A total that leaves the range and comes back into it is the exact sum, with no violation. A one-operator `map` step is computed wide too, and a step out of range is still a violation. `@vectorize` now holds on such kernels; a checked `bounded_kernel_u32` fold went from 88 ms to 54 ms locally, against 52 ms for its `wrapping_*` form. A product, an `Int` or `U64` sum, a sum over a `vec` or a range, and a loop that prints or calls keep their per-element check.
