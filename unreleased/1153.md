### DNA: a Review takes its signers from the route (GH #1089)

- **Changed:** when the organization opens a Review, it records the positions `hale dna route` names for the Review's candidate (the Board, for a proposal), with their holders, and the gates guarding the change (`review.routed`). After that, a verdict from anyone who holds none of those positions is refused, whatever authority it claims, and approval needs every required position. A route that left some path uncovered also requires the Review's own authority, and holders are routed again while the Review is open. A Review whose route names no held position keeps its own required authority.
- **Added:** `hale dna review <id> approve --evidence <receipt>` cites a filed gate run (`{kind: "gate.run", gate, sha, conclusion}`). A Review with gates settles approved only with a passing run at the candidate. A run at another commit is refused as stale, and a run that did not pass as rejected.
