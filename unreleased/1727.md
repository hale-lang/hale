### The habitat: forge and identity are allocated roles

- **Breaking:** `hale dna github sync` and the `dna.github` setting are gone. Use `hale dna forge sync`, and allocate the forge with `hale habitat forge <organism> github --repo <owner/name> [--key-vault <slot>]` (the head's `Commands::allocate_forge`, the Board's). `dna.github.board` stays.
- `forge` and `identity` are roles the habitat allocates, as it allocates memory: each is a `habitat.allocated` row naming its provider. The forge is `none` until the Board allocates one; the identity is the habitat's own sign-in. `Commands::allocations` lists both, and a graft takes `--forge <provider>`.
- A GitHub forge names its repository and the vault slot of its token, never the token. `hale dna forge sync` goes through the allocated forge; with none allocated it answers `forge: no forge allocated`.
