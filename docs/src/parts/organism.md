# The organism

An **organism** is your application together with the parts that let it be governed, changed, run and watched. Its code can change itself, and you hold the door. A change is proposed in a sandbox, proven with the toolchain from the earlier parts, shown to whoever has the authority to decide it, applied exactly as approved, expressed on your machine or a fleet, and watched until it holds or rolls back. Every step is a row in a record kept in your repository, so anyone with a clone has the whole story.

The organism is drawn as parts, each with one job, one owner and one way of failing:
- the **genome** it is made of;
- the **record** and working **memory**;
- the **nerves** between its parts;
- the **spine** that decides;
- the **head** and **face** where people reach it;
- the **legs**, their **hat**, **hands** and **voice**, which do the work;
- the **heart**, your application;
- the **body** it runs on;
- the **senses** and **reflexes** that watch it;
- the **skin** that keeps its secrets.

[The organism, part by part](../dna/index.md) draws the map. Each part then has a chapter of its own.

All of it is written in Hale, with the language, the locus model and the claims of the earlier parts. The organism's own law is a constitution its build checks ([Constitutions](../constitutions.md)). Its parts are loci on a typed bus. What it may reach is proven before it runs.

**DNA** is the building block the organism is assembled from: `vendor/dna`, the `dna::Dna` locus and the `hale dna` commands. It comes last in this part ([DNA, the building block](../dna/dna.md)), because it is the inside view. You can run and govern an organism without opening it.

**What the next part adds.** An organism looks after one application and its record. [The habitat](./habitat.md) is the design for what several organisms and the people in them share: isolated records, deliberate sharing between them, who may act and on whose authority, and the standard equipment an organism grows with. It is a design, not yet the tree.
