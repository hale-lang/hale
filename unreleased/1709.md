### DNA faces

- A genome declares its faces in `hale.toml` (`[dna.faces.<name>]`: `application`, `consumes`, `acts_as`, `url`); one that names none has the standard face, which the toolchain ships. The standard face serves the browser shell and the attached project's `ui/` under `/app/` and passes every other request to the head, which now serves surfaces only: its static files and `/app/` are gone. `hale dna head` and `dna/face/start.sh` start the face on `--port` (8792) with the head behind it on `--head-port` (8796); the head takes no webroot argument.
