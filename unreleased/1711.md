### Survey

- The allocation survey no longer reads a call of a method of `self` as carrying every field: it carries the fields the method's body reads. A `String` field replaced whole by a value built from such a call (`self.error = "load: " + rows.err.kind` where `rows` came from a `self` method that never reads `error`) is store-latest and not reported. A method that reads the field, directly or through a method it calls, keeps the replace an append.
