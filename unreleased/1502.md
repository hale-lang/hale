- A `String` field replaced with values of varying length no longer grows: its blocks come from size classes and a retired one is reused up to its real capacity (1M replaces at lengths 6–55: 36 MB → 4.8 MB, 1.1 s → 0.08 s), and a struct replaced whole gets the same. The memory-bound survey models such a replace as store-latest, and records a `String` concatenation however it is spelled, not only one with a literal in it (GH #1490).
