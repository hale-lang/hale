- `probes { http(PORT); }` on the main locus serves the process's health over HTTP from a runtime thread of its own: `/healthz` (503 naming any queue that holds work its worker has not touched for 10 s, `LOTUS_PROBES_STALL_MS`), `/readyz` (ready once the main locus's births are done, 503 while draining) and `/capacity` (each queue's waiting cells, capacity and stall time, as JSON). A program without the block serves and samples nothing.
