- A seed declares the web UIs it serves in `hale.toml` (`[dna.ui.<name>]`: `label`, `service` the compose service that serves it, `path` under its address, `/` by default, and `login`, a declared secret with `person = "read"` to sign in with). The operator face's home screen lists the web UIs across every organism in place of its bare organisms list: each organism's face, and each declared UI linked at the address its services allocation publishes for that service (or `loopback only on <host>`), with `hale habitat organism <organism> dna secret show <login>` as how to sign in, never the value. The head answers them as `Commands::uis`, a read; `hale dna declared-uis [dir]` prints a declaration, and `hale habitat graft` refuses a manifest whose UIs are refused, saying why.
