### Memory's DSNs carry no password (GH #989, PR #1239)

- **Breaking:** a role's DSN names the vault entry that holds its password, `postgres://<role>@host:port/db?sslmode=…&vault=postgres-<role>`, and pq presents that entry on the wire during SASL. `hale dna memory migrate` prints DSNs of this form. A DSN with a password in it no longer connects: take the password out and name the entry with `vault=`.
