- An organism grafted with the habitat's defaults (`nerves = reuse`) is an account of its own on the habitat's NATS (`ORG_<key>`, its users keyed by its record) instead of a NATS server of its own: one less container per organism. Each organization a nerves server holds is two files of its `nerves.d/` (its account, and its secrets, mode 600), which the server's `nats.conf` includes. Removing the organism drops its account and its stream's data there and keeps the server; `--own nerves` still gives it a server of its own. An organization's users are named for its record (`spine-<key>`, `owner-<key>`, …): an organization a toolchain earlier than this one wrote into a clone connects as `spine` and must be updated (`hale dna upgrade` says how).
- Each of a habitat's allocations (its Postgres, NATS and senses' store) and its forge has a memory limit, several times what it holds at rest.
