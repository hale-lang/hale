### Changed

- A heart granted the `broker` heart role may run JetStream work queues on its organism's nerves server. Its two users, `heart-<id>` and `heart-<id>-node`, are now an account of the heart's own (`HEART_<ID>`) with JetStream, beside the organization's account rather than in it. The heart may publish `$JS.API.>` to create and run its streams and consumers. Its nodes may pull (`$JS.API.CONSUMER.MSG.NEXT.*.*`), ack (`$JS.ACK.>`) and subscribe their inbox `heart.<id>._INBOX.>`; they cannot create, change, read or delete a stream or consumer. JetStream's API covers the whole account, so the heart's own account is the boundary: the heart reaches only its own streams, never DNA's. Its subjects, users and vault slots are unchanged, and `hale habitat migrate` or `hale dna upgrade` rewrites the account and restarts the nerves. Voice's queue routing needs this when voice is grafted into a habitat.
