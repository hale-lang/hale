- The habitat's own machine answers to its own name: with a habitat on another machine (`up --host rack-a.lan`), `hale habitat organism place … --host rack-a.lan` (or `--host rack-a`) and `graft --host` mean the same as `--host local`, and their output names the machine instead of `local`. A placement there is still recorded as `local`; its default `{id}` is the name's first label.
