- **`require no_silent_loss(topic T | all G)`**: a claim that no route on a topic, or on every topic of a group, loses a message silently. It reads every boundary on the wire subject (an `on_unmatched` swallow, a `drop_old`/`drop_new` shed, a `send` or `discard`, a binding whose loss is `drop`); a refusal the caller sees is not silent, and a custom refusal handler, a supervised connect-side binding or a hole over the route are uncertified. The configuration half of GH #1327 §2.
