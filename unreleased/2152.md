### Breaking

- A constant of an imported seed, read through its alias (`lib::LIMIT`), has its declared type. It was untyped, so its value was held to no type: `let s: String = lib::LIMIT` of an `Int` constant built, and `s` held the `Int`. It is now refused ("let `s`: expected `String`, got `Int`"). Declare the type the constant has, or convert it (`to_string(lib::LIMIT)`).

### Fixed

- A field written through a capacity cell (`let cell = self.entries.acquire(); cell.key = ...`) is checked as the pool element's field. A write of the wrong type, or of a field the element does not declare, checked and the build refused it; `hale check` refuses it now, with the build's reason.

### Changed

- internal: the build reads an assignment's target's type from the checker; its own typing of a place is deleted (GH #2111).
