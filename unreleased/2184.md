Breaking: none

### Fixed

- An `async_io` pool no longer stops draining its inbox when the round of a timed loop takes longer than its own sleep (`while !self.started { work; sleep(1ms) }`). The pool's expiry sweep read the clock before each resume, so two such loops passed the worker back and forth inside one sweep, and the cell that ends them was never drained. The sweep now reads the clock once and resumes each coroutine at most once; a park made during it waits for the next pass, after a cell (GH #2183).
