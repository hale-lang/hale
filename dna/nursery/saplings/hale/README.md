# The hale organism

The organism that works on the Hale toolchain and the repositories beside
it. This repository is its genome. The code it works on is its codebases:
`hale-lang/hale` and `hale-lang/voice`, attached when the habitat grafts it
(`hale habitat graft hale`), from the `[[dna.codebase]]` list in
`hale.toml`. Its positions and their mandates are the nursery's
`hale-organism` pack, proposed to its Board at the graft.
