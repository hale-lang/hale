- `std::http::Server` serves its connections concurrently when it is placed on an `async_io` pool (`placement { web: cooperative(pool = web) where async_io; }`): each connection is its own coroutine, bounded by `max_connections` (256), and `shutdown()` drains the requests in flight. Five concurrent one-second requests now take one second, not five. Elsewhere it serves one at a time, as before.
